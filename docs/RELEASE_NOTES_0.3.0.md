## New

- ClashPilot donates to your clan. Every few minutes it opens the clan chat and fills your clanmates' requests: exactly what they asked for when they picked the troops, and dragons, balloons, giants and lightning spells when they left the choice to you. Donations are paid in elixir and dark elixir, never in gems. It is a switch on the General page, on to start with.
- Capital Gold is collected. When its bubble shows over your village, ClashPilot taps it, collects the gold and closes the window. It is a switch on the General page.
- Treasure chests are opened. When a chest turns up, ClashPilot taps it open and takes the reward.
- After a battle during an event, ClashPilot presses Claim Reward before going home, so the event's reward is no longer left behind.

## Fixed

- On bigger Town Halls the walls could be missed in the upgrade list. ClashPilot now looks further down the list and finds them there.
- On bigger Town Halls an upgrade costing millions could be read as costing thousands. Prices in the millions are now read in full.

## Changed

- When laying a line of troops, the finger rests a moment longer before it moves, so a line is less likely to move the camera instead of dropping troops.

## Install

1. Download `ClashPilot-0.3.0.zip` below and unzip it into a folder of its own. Everything it saves goes there.
2. Open `ClashPilot.exe`. Windows will say the app comes from an unknown publisher the first time: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your MuMu Player instance on the **Settings** page, then press **Start**.

Updating from an earlier version: unzip into the same folder and replace `ClashPilot.exe`. Your settings, accounts and statistics stay where they are.

Needs Windows 10 or 11 and MuMu Player with Clash of Clans installed, signed in and past the tutorial. Help and questions: [Discord](https://discord.gg/mjanFJ8arv).
